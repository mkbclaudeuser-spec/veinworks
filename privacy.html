Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Who we are

Veinworks is a small apparel brand that sells hand-drawn sigil designs and posts a short animated story series on TikTok.

Our TikTok app (Veinworks Poster)

Veinworks Poster is a private tool. Only the Veinworks owner signs in with it, and it posts only to the Veinworks TikTok account. When the owner signs in, the tool receives from TikTok:

  • basic profile information of the signed-in account (display name and account ID), to confirm it is the right account;
  • permission to publish videos to that account (the video.publish scope).

The access token is stored only on the owner's computer. We do not collect, sell, or share data about any other TikTok user. We do not read viewers' data, messages, or followers.

Store customers

Orders are taken and fulfilled through our store at veinworks.printify.me, run on Printify. Payment and shipping data you enter there is handled by Printify and its payment partners under the store's privacy policy. Veinworks sees only what is needed to fulfill your order.

This website

This site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, ads, or tracking on it. GitHub may keep standard server logs.

Removing data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok settings, and we delete the stored token when access ends.
